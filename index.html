Learn Business Chinese Advanced with Phoebe

A message that is perfectly polite in an email is stiff on WeChat, and a word-for-word translation of polite English is too casual for both. Six sessions for people who already speak everyday Chinese and need it to work in business: register as a choice made per channel, the shape of a formal email and a business WeChat, presenting numbers with 同比 (tóngbǐ, year on year) and 环比 (huánbǐ, period on period), negotiation and face, and business dining. Every Chinese word on every page carries its pinyin and English. A formality checker in your browser finds the casual, formal and over-formal words in a draft and scores it against the band its channel expects.

The six sessions 🎯

The running case is Lumen Logistics, an invented Singapore freight company, 鲁门物流 (Lǔmén Wùliú, Lumen Logistics), opening a partnership with a Shanghai distributor. You are the person they send.

🎚️ Session 1 · core

Register: the same sentence, three ways

Person, question form and company: the three levers that move a sentence from a colleague chat to a formal email, and written language versus speech.

▶ Start here ✉️ Session 2 · core

Formal emails and WeChat

The blocks of a formal email, the shape of a business WeChat, subject lines, and why 此致 敬礼 (cǐzhì jìnglǐ, with respect) is a convention and not a standard.

 🎛️ Session 3 · bench night

The formality checker

One message written five ways, scored against three channels. Each draft fits one; three swaps fix a literal translation; polishing everything overshoots even email.

★ the bench 📊 Session 4 · advanced

Presenting numbers

同比 (tóngbǐ, year on year) and 环比 (huánbǐ, period on period) as the statistics bureau defines them, growth, decline and share, and a result always said with its comparison.

 ⚖️ Session 5 · advanced

Negotiation and face

Face as Graham and Lam describe it, reading an indirect no, and trading concessions toward a clear next step.

 🥂 Session 6 · capstone

Business dining, and the capstone

Seating, toasts and polite refusal, then the full negotiation and both follow-ups, checked. Final scorecard.
